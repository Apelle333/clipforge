# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1

Initial CLIPFORGE source release, adapted from AutoClip.

- Local faster-whisper transcription, Ollama candidate scoring and MP4 export.
- Word corrections, caption presets, safe fit and manual static framing.
- Grounded title fallback, sentence-boundary and overlap checks, scoring audits.
- Explicit rendered preview, seek control and numeric trimming.
- Windows install/launch/update scripts, source CI and experimental Compose.
- Static-path, browser-origin/host and upload/container validation hardening.

Alpha limitations: speech accuracy and subjective ranking require review;
live CSS preview approximates ASS. No accounts, community marketplace,
social publishing or public multi-user hosting.
